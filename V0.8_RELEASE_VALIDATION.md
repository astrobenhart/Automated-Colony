# v0.8 Release Validation Report

Date: 2026-06-27
Branch: v0.8-development

## Executive Summary

FAIL

v0.8 is feature complete, and the first generational loop is functioning: births occur, children enter later generations, natural deaths occur, households persist, families persist, and succession events are recorded. However, all three 100-year autonomous runs ended in population collapse under default settings. The release should not merge until this is converted into a GitHub issue and resolved or explicitly accepted as a release-blocking balance failure.

Recommendation: Requires additional development before release.

## Stability

No simulation stalls were detected in any run. No extinction occurred, and there was no runaway population growth. The 50-year runs remained playable but showed a downward trend, especially seed 8102. The 100-year runs consistently collapsed below the validation threshold, ending between 10 and 15 villagers.

## Generational Continuity

Births occurred in all runs, natural aging deaths occurred, and generation counts reached 2-3. Families never disappeared incorrectly, and households persisted across founders. Household succession was active, with 15-40 succession events depending on run length.

The major concern is renewal strength. Births did not keep pace with natural deaths over 100 years. Household split events remained at 0 in all runs, suggesting the residential growth model is not exercising household division under default long-run conditions.

## Economy

No irreversible food or water collapse was detected by the validation harness. Stored food and water ended at 0, but local food and water remained available and settlement status reported Stable. Housing capacity remained above final population in every run, so the observed decline was not caused by a late housing shortage.

## Renderer

Warm cached renderer performance remained stable, around 3.4-4.3 ms per sampled frame after long simulations. Cold terrain cache build time remained around 1.1-2.1 seconds, which is acceptable for a cold sample but should continue to be tracked. Peak process memory stayed around 70-81 MB across long runs, with no obvious leak signal from this sample set.

## Chronicle

The Chronicle remained active throughout long runs. The 50-year runs produced 608-670 entries; the 100-year runs produced 1228-1295 entries. Environmental events were frequent and family/succession entries continued to appear. No Chronicle inactivity was observed, though event volume may be worth reviewing later for readability.

## Bugs Discovered

1. Release blocker: 100-year default simulations consistently fall into population collapse.
2. Major balance/design concern: births are too low relative to aging deaths over century-scale runs.
3. Major behavior concern: household split events remained at 0 across all validation runs, so family-driven settlement expansion may not be triggering under default conditions.

## Balance Observations

The likely tuning target is not resource production or housing capacity. Resources remained reachable and housing capacity exceeded population. The pressure appears to sit in renewal: partnership/birth cadence, mature second-generation partnership formation, adult household splitting, or eligibility rules that make later births too sparse.

Prefer a narrow issue and targeted tuning pass over new systems.
