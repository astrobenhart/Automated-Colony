# v0.8 Release Validation Report

Date: 2026-07-01
Branch: v0.8-development
Validation path: `SimulationRunner` -> `World.update()`

## Executive Summary

FAIL

This validation used the headless tick-faithful runner. The completed runs executed the production gameplay update path through `World.update()`. Rendering, UI, frame limiting, and presentation delays were disabled; simulation ticks were not skipped.

The latest balance pass materially improves population renewal. The completed 100-year run survives, remains above the starting population, grows into a mature village, and avoids extinction. The old failure mode of peaceful villages slowly collapsing below founding population is not present in the completed century run.

However, the requested validation suite could not be completed:

- the requested 5 x 100-year batch timed out after 6 hours with no output artifact
- a single 100-year run for seed 9301 completed, but took 7.65 hours
- the requested 2 x 200-year suite was not attempted after the century runtime made it clear the full matrix would not finish in a practical validation window

Recommendation: Requires further development

The balance direction looks promising, but v0.8 is not ready to merge until the full validation matrix can complete or the release criteria are adjusted. Validation throughput is now itself a release concern.

## Population Behaviour

Completed evidence shows a believable but heavy population curve for seed 9301:

- Early expansion: population rises rapidly from 45 to 75 by year 5.
- Young settlement growth: population reaches 96-102 by years 20-40.
- Mature settlement plateau: population remains near 90-100 through years 45-80.
- Late-century decline: population falls from 92 at year 80 to 62 at year 100.
- Long-term status: final population remains above the starting population, but the village is past its peak.

This is not extinction and not runaway exponential growth. It is a strong early expansion followed by a mature plateau and a late-century contraction. That shape is plausible, but one seed is not enough to declare the release stable.

## Remaining Issues

Severity 1 - Release-blocking:

- Full final validation could not be completed. The requested 5 x 100-year and 2 x 200-year suite is currently too slow to run to completion in a practical release-validation window.

Severity 2 - Validation confidence:

- Only one 100-year seed completed under the current balance settings. That seed looks demographically healthier, but it is not enough evidence to prove the release target.

Severity 2 - Performance:

- Headless simulation speed drops heavily as the village grows. Population and household growth now appear to expose CPU scaling costs in the tick-faithful runner.

Severity 3 - Economy/residential watch items:

- Stored food remains at 0 in the completed runs, though local food prevents a collapse warning.
- Homeless and overcrowded households remain in the completed 100-year final state despite substantial construction.

## Recommendation

Requires further development.

Do not merge v0.8 into main yet.

Recommended next action:

Investigate headless simulation performance under larger mature villages. The balance appears closer to the intended design, but the release cannot be certified until the requested long-run matrix completes. Focus on profiling mature-village CPU cost without changing gameplay behavior.
