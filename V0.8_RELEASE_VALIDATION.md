# v0.8 Release Validation Report

Date: 2026-06-28
Branch: v0.8-development
Validation path: `SimulationRunner` -> `World.update()`

## Executive Summary

FAIL

The final v0.8 validation used the headless tick-faithful runner. Every validation run executed the production gameplay update path through `World.update()`. Rendering, UI, frame limiting, and presentation delays were disabled; simulation ticks were not skipped.

The partnership lifecycle fix improved the renewal loop:

- partnered adults establish shared households
- household splits occur
- residential construction and expansion continue
- families persist into later generations
- partner references are cleared after death
- no housing, food, water, construction, planner, or simulation deadlocks were observed
- Chronicle activity remains healthy

However, v0.8 is not ready to merge. The 50-year runs are broadly stable, but all three 100-year runs decline substantially from the starting population. The settlement survives, but peaceful villages are not yet maintaining stable or growing century-scale renewal.

Recommendation: Requires further development

## Population Renewal

The renewal loop is active but too weak over a century.

Confirmed functioning:

- births continue after the founder generation
- children age into adults
- households split
- homes are constructed and expanded
- families persist and reach generation 4
- household succession continues
- no demographic deadlock or extinction occurred

Remaining failure:

- births do not replace natural deaths over 100 years
- population peaks during early/mid settlement growth and then trends downward
- late-century settlements retain abundant housing and resources but too few children and working adults

This indicates that the prior architectural blocker was improved, but the final release blocker is not fully resolved. The next investigation should focus on why later-generation birth opportunities remain insufficient despite functioning co-residential households and abundant housing.

## Economy

Food, water, and housing did not cause the decline.

- Food remained positive in all final states.
- Water remained positive in all final states.
- Housing capacity greatly exceeded population in every 100-year run.
- Homeless households were not reported.
- Overcrowded households were not reported.
- Construction continued across long simulations.

Economy conclusion: the population decline is demographic, not caused by resource collapse or residential capacity failure.

## Chronicle

The Chronicle remained active throughout validation.

- 50-year runs produced 644-697 entries.
- 100-year runs produced 1189-1281 entries.
- Entries continued to include births, deaths, family history, household succession, and environmental events.

Chronicle quality is acceptable for release validation. No Chronicle inactivity was observed.

## Remaining Issues

Severity 1 - Release-blocking:

- Century-scale population renewal remains below target. Peaceful settlements survive but decline from 45 starting population to 22-29 after 100 years.

Severity 2 - Balance/investigation:

- Later-generation births remain too low relative to natural deaths even after the partnership lifecycle fix.
- The exact remaining limiter should be identified before tuning. Candidate areas include partnership availability, partner age distribution, child spacing, dependent-child caps, birth scoring, and late-generation household composition.

Severity 3 - Validation performance:

- Headless validation is accurate but slow. Single-worker 100-year runs took roughly 52-80 minutes on this machine.
- This does not affect gameplay correctness, but future release validation would benefit from simulation performance optimization.

## Recommendation

Requires further development

Do not merge v0.8 into main yet.

Recommended next action:

Run a focused late-generation birth eligibility investigation using the tick-faithful runner. Do not tune births or deaths until the remaining limiter is identified. The architecture is now functioning well enough to validate real demographic balance, but the current balance does not yet satisfy the v0.8 release principle that peaceful villages naturally renew and expand across generations.
