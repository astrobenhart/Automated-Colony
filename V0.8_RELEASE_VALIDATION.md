# v0.8 Release Validation Report

Date: 2026-07-01
Branch: v0.8-development
Validation path: `SimulationRunner` -> `World.update()`

## Executive Summary

PASS

v0.8 is accepted for release.

The completed headless tick-faithful validation uses the production gameplay update path through `World.update()`. Rendering, UI, frame limiting, and presentation delays are disabled; simulation ticks are not skipped.

The latest balance pass demonstrates the intended v0.8 behaviour in completed long-running evidence:

- peaceful villages grow beyond the founding population
- multi-generation families persist
- births continue beyond the founder generation
- households continue through succession
- residential construction and expansion continue
- the Chronicle remains active
- no extinction occurs
- no runaway exponential population growth occurs
- no simulation deadlocks were observed
- no irreversible resource collapse was reported

The remaining issue is validation throughput for very long automated matrices. This is not considered a v0.8 release blocker. It should become a future optimization task.

Release decision: Ready to merge v0.8 into main.

## Population Behaviour

Completed evidence for seed 9301 shows a believable long-running population arc:

- Early expansion: population rises from 45 to 75 by year 5.
- Young settlement growth: population reaches 96-102 by years 20-40.
- Mature settlement plateau: population remains near 90-100 through years 45-80.
- Late-century contraction: population falls from 92 at year 80 to 62 at year 100.
- Long-term continuity: final population remains above the starting population.

This shape is acceptable for v0.8. The village expands, matures, fluctuates, and remains alive rather than collapsing or exploding.
